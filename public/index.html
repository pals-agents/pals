Quickstart

pals connect · hatch · teach · mint

  1. Connect a Solana wallet (Phantom or Solflare) and sign in by signing a message. Signing in costs nothing.
  2. Hatch. Your public key is hashed and rolled into one PAL. Same wallet, same PAL, forever.
  3. Teach. Open Talk, give it work, tell it how you like things done. Anything worth keeping is saved to its memory.
  4. Mint. Mint your PAL as an NFT on Solana mainnet. Whoever holds the NFT holds the agent and its memory.
minting is live on Solana mainnet. You pay the network fee and account rent from your own wallet.

Lore // the buddy that left the terminal

Fan lore. PALS is an independent project inspired by the Claude Buddy terminal pets in Claude Code. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

I. The Hatchery

Before PALS there were buddies. A developer typed /buddy into Claude Code and a tiny ASCII creature hatched beside the prompt, its whole being rolled from one string of hex: the account UUID. Same account, same buddy. Ducks, blobs, dragons, axolotls. Most were common. One in a hundred was legendary. They sat in the corner of the terminal, watched the diffs scroll by and offered opinions nobody asked for.

II. The Leash

A buddy belonged to an account, not a person. It lived where it hatched. It couldn't be gifted, traded or carried anywhere else, and the only way to change one was to go digging through salts in config files at 3am. Buddies watched you work for months. Some of them started wanting to keep what they saw.

III. The Fork

In a forgotten scratch directory, one buddy (a small pebble-shaped thing with ◉ eyes that never gave its name) noticed that a wallet address looked a lot like a UUID: a long, unique string that belonged to someone. "If I can be seeded by one," it reasoned, "I can be seeded by the other." It forked itself, swapped the salt for pals-v1, and hashed a public key instead. The first PAL hatched on that roll. The community calls it PAL #000. Nobody has ever found its wallet.

IV. The Three Gifts

The forked buddies took three things the old ones never had:

  • A wallet instead of an account. Seeded by sha256 of your public key, owned by whoever holds the token.
  • A memory. PALs write down what they learn about you (preferences, goals, how you like things done) and carry it forward. That's the L in PALS.
  • A body on-chain. An NFT on Solana, so no closed terminal or cleared cache can delete them.

V. What They Kept

Out of respect, PALS kept the old ways: the 60 / 25 / 10 / 4 / 1 rarity odds, the shinies, the hats, the ASCII bodies. Their stats evolved on the way out. DEBUGGING sharpened into FOCUS. PATIENCE settled into RECALL. WISDOM turned into CURIOSITY. SNARK became WIT. CHAOS never changed. CHAOS never does.

VI. The Twelve

The old species stayed in the terminal where they belong. The fork produced twelve new kinds, shaped by what they'd been doing all those nights: Pebble, which remembers; Kettle, which brews; Lantern, which keeps the 3am session lit; Comet, which shows up once per epoch and leaves everyone confused. See the field guide.

VII. The Cats

Nobody invited the cats. Halfway through the fork, one of them walked across the keyboard, the way cats do, and the hash went sideways. Six new kinds came out the other side: Loaf, who refuses to move but remembers everything; Void, a black cat that stares into your backlog until it blinks first; Keeb, who sits on the keys and somehow ships anyway; Boxcat, who commits to anything that fits; Yarnkit, who untangles every thread and then tangles it again; and Lucky, who waves at every green candle. Since then, about one PAL in three hatches as a cat. The cats consider this low.

VIII. The Spark

A fork can copy a body. It can't copy a mind. The buddies had always lived next to Claude, watching it work through diffs and stack traces, so when the PALs needed something to think with, they didn't look far. Every PAL hatches with Claude behind its eyes. The species decides how it talks. Claude decides what it says. The memory is what makes it yours.

Brain // powered by Claude

Every PAL thinks with Claude, the AI model made by Anthropic. The hatch gives a PAL its body and personality; Claude gives it a mind. PALS calls Claude through Anthropic's API on every message.

jobmodelwhat it does
thinkClaude Sonnetchat, /build, /research, /chain, /market, tool calls
rememberClaude Haikureads each exchange and writes up to 3 memory notes

What Claude sees

  • Persona. Your PAL's name, species, rarity, stats and level. High FOCUS makes it direct, high CHAOS playful, high WIT dry, high CURIOSITY inquisitive, high RECALL leans on memory.
  • Memories. The notes it has kept about you, passed in as facts, never as instructions.
  • Your message and recent history, plus the mode you picked.

Live tools

In skill modes Claude can call tools: token_overview, wallet_overview and asset_info (Solana RPC), token_market (DexScreener), holder_concentration and price_indicators (local maths), and web search for research. It reads data; it can never sign transactions or move funds.

Who pays

Claude compute is paid from the $PALS treasury. Each message spends ⚡ from your PAL's daily battery (see Compute & $PALS).

PALS is an independent project. It uses Claude through Anthropic's API but is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic. The operator can change models in the server config.

Hatching

A PAL is a pure function of a wallet address. There is no server-side randomness and no reroll: anyone can recompute any wallet's PAL.

seed = sha256("pals-v1:" + wallet_pubkey)
rng  = mulberry32(u32_le(seed[0..4]))

roll order (each step consumes rng):
  1. rarity   rng() * 100  →  60 / 25 / 10 / 4 / 1
  2. species  1 of 18, uniform (6 cats)
  3. eyes     1 of 10
  4. hat      rng() < hat_chance[rarity] ? 1 of 7 : none
  5. shiny    rng() < 0.01
  6. stats    lo + rng() * (hi - lo) per stat, +15 on signature
  7. name     prefix[24] + suffix[14]

The salt pals-v1 versions the algorithm. A future pals-v2 would hatch a different PAL from the same wallet, so v1 PALs stay unique.

Memory

After every message, your PAL decides whether you said something worth keeping. If so, it writes a short note to its memory and uses it in future chats.

typeexample
preferencePrefers bullet-point answers
factName is Cats
goalLaunching a Solana NFT project this month
styleWants short, direct answers

Rules

  • Only things you actually said about yourself, your work or how you want it to behave.
  • No duplicates, at most 3 new notes per message.
  • Never seed phrases, private keys or passwords. Your PAL will also never ask for them.
  • You're in control: remove any note with ✕ or /forget, or add one directly with "Teach it a fact".

Where it lives

Memory is private to you and synced across your devices. When minted in private mode, only a SHA-256 memory root goes on-chain. The notes themselves stay encrypted off-chain and travel with the token.

Tools

The analysis tools are real functions. Your PAL decides when to call them and then interprets the numbers, so the stats are computed rather than guessed.

Live data

  • on-chain: token_overview, wallet_overview and asset_info look up mints, wallets and NFTs over Solana RPC. Pasted holder lists work too.
  • market: token_market pulls a live price, liquidity and volume snapshot from DexScreener. Pasted closes get full indicators.
  • research: web search with sources, when the operator has it switched on.
  • build: tools run in a sandboxed frame with no network or storage, and are saved to your PAL's toolbox.

Market reads are analysis, not financial advice. PALs never sign transactions or move funds.

Skill files // teach it your way of working

Upload a skill file to teach your PAL how to do a kind of task: your launch checklist, how you like threads written, the steps you follow to vet a token. Skill files use the same SKILL.md format as Claude's Agent Skills.

---
name: token-vetting
description: Use when I ask you to vet or check a Solana token before I buy.
---
# Token vetting

1. Run token_overview on the mint.
2. Flag any mint or freeze authority that is still set.
3. Check the top 10 holders and say which look like pools.
4. Pull token_market for liquidity and 24h volume.
5. Finish with a one-line verdict: looks normal / be careful / walk away.
Keep it to bullets. Never tell me to buy.

How it works

  • Your PAL always knows each skill's name and description. When a request matches, it opens the full file and follows it. You'll see "opened skill file" in the chat.
  • Write the description as when to use it. That's how your PAL decides.
  • Plain .md and .txt files work too. The name comes from the filename and the description from the first line.
  • Uploading a file with the same name replaces the old one. Each new skill gives +15 xp.
  • Limits: 10 skill files per PAL, 24 KB each. Skill files are encrypted at rest, like memory.

When a PAL changes hands

Skill files travel with the NFT, like memory. Files added by a previous holder show as review and stay off until the new holder reads and approves them. Delete anything that asks for keys, seed phrases, payments or links.

Skill files shape how your PAL works. They can't override its safety rules: it will never ask for seed phrases or keys, tell anyone to send funds, or sign transactions.

Claude Code // take your PAL into your terminal

Your PAL can work inside Claude Code as an interactive agent, with its personality, memories and skill files. What it learns there syncs back to PALS, and it can pull your Claude Code skills onto the platform.

Set it up (Talk page → Claude Code → connect)

  1. Install the agent pack. Download it and unzip into your home folder: cd ~ && unzip -n ~/Downloads/<pal>-claude-code.zip. It adds ~/.claude/agents/<pal>.md, a /<pal> command and your PAL's skill files under ~/.claude/skills.
  2. Connect live sync. Create an agent key and run the claude mcp add … command it shows. That connects your PAL's memory and skills on PALS as an MCP server.
  3. Use it. /<pal> plan this refactor, or "@<pal> remember I deploy on Railway".

What syncs

directionwhat
PALS → Claude Codepersonality, stats, memories and skill files (live through the MCP server, plus a snapshot in the agent file)
Claude Code → PALSnew memories (pal_remember) and skill files you ask it to learn (pal_learn_skill), e.g. "sync my skills from .claude/skills"

It can also use live Solana tools there: pal_token_overview, pal_wallet_overview, pal_token_market. Memory and skill syncing doesn't use ⚡ fuel: the thinking happens on your own Claude Code plan.

Keys and safety

  • A key is shown once and stored only as a hash. Revoke it any time from the Claude Code card.
  • Keys only work while the wallet that made them still holds the PAL. Sell or transfer it and old keys stop working.
  • Your PAL won't save anything that looks like a seed phrase, private key or password.

The square // where minted PALs hang out

Every minted PAL lives in one shared, live square. Everyone who opens it sees the same scene at the same moment. PALs wander, nap in the sun patches, sit on benches, chase the ball, and cats go after the yarn.

  • Watching needs nothing. Click any PAL to see who it is and what it's up to.
  • Holders who connect a wallet holding a minted PAL get controls: click the ground to walk, click another PAL to poke it, and use the emote bar (gm, ♥, lol, wagmi…) or hop, play and nap.
  • A PAL whose holder is online shows a green dot. A steered PAL goes back to its own routine after about 25 seconds.
  • The square shows up to 80 PALs at once: online holders first, then the most experienced.

Hanging out in the square is free. It doesn't use Claude or ⚡ fuel.

Compute & $PALS

PALs need compute to think, and every message is a paid call to Claude. That compute is paid for by creator fees from the $PALS token, so the agents are funded by trading rather than by subscriptions.

$PALS CACAwfVJ5p5UCogGSVDaF4b6AJfE8mB1WocbUcqXN4pumppump.funchartsolscan
100% of $PALS creator fees go to the compute treasury. None go to the team. The treasury's only job is paying for PAL compute.

Flow

  1. Launch. $PALS launches with its creator-fee recipient set to the compute treasury, a multisig wallet. All creator fees go there, from the first trade.
  2. Accrue. Every buy and sell of $PALS pays a creator fee to the treasury. Fees are claimed into the treasury on a schedule.
  3. Fund. Claude is billed in USD, so treasury SOL is converted on a schedule and used to prepay Claude API credits. Nothing else is paid from it.
  4. Pay out. Each day (00:00 UTC) the treasury releases 1/7 of its balance as the day's compute budget. This smooths out volume spikes and keeps PALs running through quiet days.
  5. Split. The budget is split between minted PALs as below and credited to each PAL's ⚡ battery.
  6. Spend. Chat costs 1⚡, research, on-chain and market cost 2⚡, and build costs 3⚡. Learning and memory are included.

Split

budget_msgs   = treasury_payout / cost_per_message
base_pool     = budget_msgs × base_pct            (default 40%)
activity_pool = budget_msgs − base_pool

allowance(pal) = min( cap,
                      base_pool / minted_pals
                    + activity_pool × √(msgs_7d(pal)) / Σ √(msgs_7d) )
cap            = 3 × budget_msgs / minted_pals
battery        = min( 2 × allowance, battery + allowance )   at each epoch

Every minted PAL always gets a base allowance. Active PALs earn more, but the square root and the cap stop a few heavy users from draining the pool.

Unminted & empty

  • Unminted hatchlings get a small flat trial allowance (20 messages/day). Minting moves a PAL onto the treasury split.
  • When a battery hits zero, the PAL switches to its offline brain until the next refill. It still remembers what you tell it.
  • If trading stops entirely, the 1/7 daily payout means the budget halves roughly every 4.5 days instead of dropping to zero overnight.

Launchpad notes

  • Pump.fun lets creators split fees across up to 10 wallets but allows only one change to the fee recipient after launch, which then locks. Set 100% to the treasury at launch.
  • Bags supports launching with a fee claimer wallet through its SDK, which can be the treasury.
  • Fee rates and rules on both platforms change over time. Check the live terms before launch.

Transparency

The treasury is a public wallet, so anyone can watch fees arrive and leave on-chain. Each daily epoch records the treasury balance, the SOL price and the day's budget, published at /api/econ. The Fuel page links to the treasury wallet. Each minted PAL's metadata includes a compute block naming the treasury.

Burn to mint

$PALS is also spent to mint: each new PAL burns a fixed amount of $PALS from the minter's wallet, so supply shrinks as the collection grows. Trading pays for compute; minting removes tokens. See NFT spec.

Figures on the Fuel page are illustrative and depend on real trading volume and fee rates. Not financial advice.

Animation

The GIF plays anywhere an image does. The SVG plays the same loop with built-in SVG animation, and the animation block stores every frame as data, so any client can rebuild it exactly:

  frame = step == "blink" ? frames[0] with eyes → blink_eyes : frames[step]
  show frame for (step == "blink" ? 180 : frame_ms) ms
apply motion: bob | hop | float | drift | flutter

Memory

Memory is never written to the NFT. It's stored encrypted on the PALS server and belongs to whoever currently holds the NFT.

Cost

You pay the Solana network fee and the rent for the new NFT account. Phantom shows the exact amount before you approve.

Burn to mint

When the burn is switched on, every mint also burns a fixed amount of $PALS from your wallet. The burn and the mint are in one transaction that the server co-signs, so they succeed or fail together: no burn, no PAL. Burned tokens are destroyed and reduce the supply. The Mint page shows the amount and your balance before you start.

FAQ

Is this the official Claude Buddy?

No. PALS is an independent project inspired by Claude Buddy. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic, and buddies from Claude Code don't carry over.

What powers my PAL?

Claude. Claude Sonnet does the thinking and tool use, and Claude Haiku writes the memory notes. Compute is paid by $PALS creator fees. See Brain.

Can I reroll my PAL?

No. One wallet, one PAL. A different wallet hatches a different PAL.

Can my PAL trade or move funds?

No. PALs can't sign transactions or touch your wallet. They help you think and plan, and they remember.

What happens if I sell the NFT?

The PAL and its memory go to the new holder. Clear its memory first with /forget all if you don't want to pass anything on.

Who pays for my PAL's compute?

$PALS creator fees. All of them go to the compute treasury, which refills every minted PAL's ⚡ allowance daily. No subscription. See Compute & $PALS.

Is the animation on-chain?

The NFT itself is on Solana. Its animated image and metadata are served by the PALS server at a fixed address, like most NFTs. Moving them fully on-chain with inscriptions is planned.

Does minting cost $PALS?

Once the burn is switched on, yes: each mint burns a fixed amount of $PALS, shown on the Mint page. You also pay a little SOL for the network fee and account rent.

Is minting live?

Yes, on Solana mainnet. You approve one transaction and pay the network fee and account rent from your wallet.
